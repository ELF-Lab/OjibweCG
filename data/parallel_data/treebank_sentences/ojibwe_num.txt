Mii go ingoji niiyo-diba'iganeg ashi-aabita bi-waaseyaag.
Dibikong gii-kwanabibizowag niizh ininiwag. Bezhig gegaa gii'-nisaabaawe.
Gagwejiiwaazoowinini wa'awe gaa-dagoshing. Niibiwa daso-biboon gii-kagwejiiwaazo.
Niso-giizhik ningii-tazhitaamin gii-maniseyaang. Ningozis ningii-wiidookaag.
Gidaa-biidoonan niizh apabiwinan. Noondesewan.
Ogii-madaabiiwinaan iniwen naanan odasabiin.
Midaaso-diba'igaans gii-izhise.
Ningii-mijidwe bezhigwaatig bakwezhigan noongom eshkwaa-naawakweg.
Agoozh igiw niizh.
NIngo-giizhik gii-o-gibaakwa'waa.
Mii iw nisogon aabidaanimak. Gaawiin ganage gibitaanimasinoon.
Bizaan igo. Bezhig odaapinan.
Aabita bakweshim zhiiwi-bakwezhigaans ji-ashamiyan.
Bezhig amik gidaa-gagwe-ishkonanaa endasingin amikwiishan miinawaa ji-ondaadiziwaad.
Inzanagendaan ji-o-gizhaadigeyaan ingo-anama'e-giizhik iwidi gaa-inanoonigooyaan.
