Giiyaabi na ishpi-giizhigadinig gii-oninamaad.
Ogii-pishigonaan iw owewebanaabaan gaa-onji-bakobiisenig.
Onzaam wayiiba gii-kijigwaashkwani imaa manoominike-jiimaanensing gaa-onji-gwanabisenig.
Wewiib niisaandawebatoo. Onando-nakwetaan obiiwaabikoonsim madwesininig.
Naage waasa wii'-ani-izhi-gozi waabaninig.
Gaa-niizhwaaso-diba'iganedinig  gii-kigizhebaa-wiisini a'awe akiwenzii.
Giimooj ogii-kagiikimaan iniwe odikwezensiman imaa epiitakamigadinig.
Gii-anookii ji-naajigaadenig i'iw ojiimaan.
Ogii-nisidomaamaan iniwen waagoshan jiigaya'ii gii-ayaanigwen.
Gii-paashkikaamagadini imaa gii-miniiwizided.
Mii ji-bwaanawi'ad ji-gichiwinadiban. Ojaanimendam wii-azhegiiwed iwidi gaa-pi-onji-bazigwiid gaa-izhiwebadinig.
Waabiganikaadeni onik.
Gaa-izhi-neyaabikaanig bi-onji-agwaakawewag.
Aaniin ezhibii'igaadenig endaad.
Odadima'aan gaa-bimaasininig jiimaan.
Oganawaabandaan i'iw ziibi bimijiwaninig.
Gichi-nanaamadabi naanaagadawendang miinawaa bagosendang ji-mizhakwadinig.
Aaniin ezhi-gikinawaajibii'igaadenig endaad.
Ogii'-miikwa'aan gaa-waawiyeyaanig gaa-goda'aakwed.
Gii'-miikanaagoneshkige waa'-ondinised waabaninig.
Ogii-azhesidoon i'iw mazina'igan gaa-kibisininig.
Ogii-tebibidoon jiimaan jibwaa-niminaaweyaasininig.
Zanagi'aawag igiweg bami'aaganag giishkizhigaadenig minik debibijigewaad.
Babaamishkaa 'a akiwenzii anwaatininig.
Onzaam jiigaya'ii imaa gii-niibawi gaa-onji-nawadidenig ogiboodiyegwaazon.
